Landscape / Adjacent platform

Hytale is the broader sandbox overlap.

Hytale is a creator-oriented sandbox with community-server ambitions and Early Access beginning January 13, 2026.

Sandbox creators

A platform can be both a destination and a place where communities operate their own spaces.

Hytale is broader and more prominent than the tiny projects, but it overlaps wherever cubacadabra’s audience overlaps Luanti or Brickadia: people who want to inhabit, modify, and operate worlds.

Its community-server direction makes it relevant to the relationship between official platform and independent operator.
