Landscape / Main product comparison

Jamango is a browser-first play/create benchmark.

Jamango combines a browser-accessible multiplayer catalog with world-building, scripting, physics, and publishing workflows.

The first-five-minutes test

Study how little friction a new creator feels before they can share.

Jamango matters because it benchmarks the complete journey: find a game, start playing, become a creator, and share something playable. Its tutorials describe an actual workflow instead of only decorative world arrangement.

I did not establish that its engine is open source. That makes it useful as a product-experience comparison rather than an openness comparison.
