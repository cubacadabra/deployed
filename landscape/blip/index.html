Landscape / Main product comparison

Blip is the biggest missing direct analogue.

Formerly Cubzh, Blip presents itself as an open-source, Luau-based, cross-platform game engine and distribution platform.

Closest additional match

Treat source availability, buildability, and a usable distribution platform as separate promises.

Blip overlaps with much of cubacadabra’s proposition: integrated creation, Luau scripting, client/server game logic, multiplayer infrastructure, mobile creation, and delivery across iOS, Android, desktop, and browsers.

The qualification matters. Its public repository currently warns that the source build is not yet functional after an import from a private repository, while downloadable alpha releases and a reproducible open build are different things.
