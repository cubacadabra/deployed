Landscape / Adjacent platform

Overte is an ownership and infrastructure comparator.

Overte is an Apache-2.0 open-source social-world system with self-hosted servers, desktop/VR support, and scripting.

Independent operation

Ownership has an operations story: servers, updates, identity, and support.

Overte is useful for asking what remains usable without one central company. It shifts the comparison away from creator tooling alone and toward hosting, governance, and operational ownership.

That is a different product shape from cubacadabra, but a valuable test of what portability means in practice.
